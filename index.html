How ChefVoyage works

Four steps from a recipe you want to cook to a basket that matches it.

1

Pick your dishes

Browse country by country — Bangladeshi Kacchi, Japanese Sushi, Thai Pad Thai, Italian Pasta — and open any dish you want on the menu.

2

How to cook it opens a detailed method for 4 people, written to match the ingredients on that dish. Follow it at the stove.

Good to know

Quick answers before you start.

Where is the recipe?
Open a country, then press How to cook it on any dish. The method sits at the top of the card: timing, then the steps, then the ingredient list those steps use. Recipes are written for 4 people.
How are quantities calculated?
Every recipe is written for 4 people. We multiply each ingredient by your guest count ÷ 4, convert mixed units (g, kg, ml, L) to one base unit, add shared ingredients together, then round to a friendly amount for shopping.
Can I trust the store results?
Stores come from OpenStreetMap, so they are real places — but opening hours and stock aren't verified. Use the directions button and call ahead for hard-to-find items.
What does “Veg-friendly” mean?
It's a quick check of the ingredient list: no meat, poultry or seafood in the recipe. It can't see hidden ingredients in store-bought sauces, so always check labels if you follow a strict diet.
Is my data saved or shared?
Your picks stay in this browser only (local storage) so you can come back later. Your location is only used to search for stores and is never stored on a server by ChefVoyage.
Any keyboard shortcuts?
⌘/Ctrl + K opens quick search for any country or dish. / focuses the search box and Esc steps back.
