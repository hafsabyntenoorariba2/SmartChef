How SmartChef works

Four steps from "what should we cook?" to a full basket in your hand.

1

Pick your dishes

Browse signature dishes country by country — Bangladeshi Biryani, Japanese Sushi, Thai Pad Thai, Italian Pasta — and add as many as you like to one meal plan.

2

Set your headcount

Tell us how many people are eating. Every ingredient quantity is scaled up or down to match, so nothing runs out and nothing goes to waste.

3

Get one merged list

Shared ingredients across dishes are combined into a single shopping list. Tick off what is already in your pantry and it drops off the list.

4

Shop at real stores

Type your city or street and SmartChef locates real supermarkets near you from OpenStreetMap data, ranked from closest to farthest.
